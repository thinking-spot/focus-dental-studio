About Invisalign®

Clear Aligners, Explained

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Invisalign® treatment uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners. You wear each set for a short period, typically a week or two, before moving on to the next, gradually shifting your teeth toward their planned positions.

The aligners are nearly invisible and removable, so you can eat, brush, and floss the way you normally do.

How Treatment Works

Treatment begins with an exam and a conversation about your goals. If Invisalign is a good fit, we’ll create your treatment plan and your custom aligners. You wear each set for most of the day, typically 20 to 22 hours, and visit us periodically so we can check your progress.

When your teeth reach their new positions, a retainer helps keep them there.

Is Invisalign® Right for You?

Many people with crowded teeth, gaps between teeth, or bite concerns are good candidates for clear aligners. Whether Invisalign is the right choice depends on your teeth and your goals, and we’ll give you an honest recommendation after an exam.

What to Expect

Many patients notice some pressure or tightness when they start a new set of aligners, which usually eases within a few days. Aligners only work when they’re in your mouth, so consistency matters: wear them for most of the day, take them out to eat and to drink anything other than water, and clean your teeth before putting them back in.

Treatment length varies from person to person, and we’ll give you an estimate at your consultation.

Common Questions

Invisalign® FAQs

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment time varies from person to person, depending on your goals and how your teeth respond. Some people finish in several months, while others need a year or more. We’ll give you an estimate at your consultation.

Does Invisalign hurt?

Most people feel mild pressure or tightness for a few days when they start a new set of aligners, which is a sign they’re working. It’s usually temporary and manageable.

Can I eat and drink normally with Invisalign?

Yes. You remove your aligners to eat and to drink anything other than water, then clean your teeth before putting them back in. That means no food restrictions, but it also means the aligners only work when you wear them.

Will people notice my aligners?

Invisalign aligners are clear and fit closely over your teeth, so they’re much less noticeable than traditional braces. Many people won’t realize you’re wearing them.

Will I need a retainer after treatment?

Yes. Teeth naturally tend to shift back toward their old positions, so a retainer is an important part of keeping your new smile. We’ll explain how and when to wear yours.

How much does Invisalign cost?

The cost depends on your individual treatment plan, and some dental plans include orthodontic benefits. Call or text us and we’ll review your benefits and discuss your options, including third-party financing with CareCredit®.
