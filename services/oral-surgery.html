Our Oral Surgery Services

Careful Treatment, Clear Communication

Tooth Extractions

Sometimes a tooth can’t be saved because of severe decay, infection, a fracture, advanced gum disease, or crowding. When that’s the case, an extraction removes the tooth so it doesn’t harm your health or your neighboring teeth.

We numb the area, take our time, and talk with you beforehand about replacement options such as an implant, bridge, or denture.

Socket Preservation

After a tooth is removed, the bone that supported it can shrink and change shape. Socket preservation places bone graft material in the empty socket to help maintain the bone, which can make it easier to place a dental implant or bridge later.

Implant Placement

A dental implant replaces the root of a missing tooth. A small post is placed in the jawbone, where it bonds with the bone over time and creates a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture. Implants are a long-term solution for missing teeth.

We place and restore dental implants at Focus Dental Studio. See our restorative dentistry page for the crowns, bridges, and dentures that attach to implants.

PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)

PRF is made from a small sample of your own blood, drawn during your visit and processed to concentrate the platelets and growth factors that support healing. It is placed in the treatment area after procedures like extractions and implant placement to help the site heal.

Because it comes from your own blood, PRF is a natural way to support your recovery.

Comfort and Recovery

After your procedure, you’ll receive clear instructions for a smooth recovery, and we’re a call or text away if you have questions.

Common Questions

Oral Surgery FAQs

Will my extraction hurt?

We numb the area before an extraction, so most patients feel pressure but not pain during the procedure. Some soreness afterward is normal, and we’ll give you instructions to help you stay comfortable.

What should I expect after a tooth extraction?

Most people feel better within a few days, though the site takes longer to heal fully. Eating soft foods, avoiding straws and smoking, and following our aftercare instructions help protect the area as it heals.

Do I need to replace a tooth after it’s removed?

In most cases, yes. A gap can allow neighboring teeth to shift and make chewing more difficult. Implants, bridges, and dentures are all options, and we’ll help you decide what fits your needs.

Are dental implants right for me?

It depends on your overall health, your gums, and the condition of your jawbone. We’ll examine your mouth and talk through whether an implant is a good option or whether another solution would serve you better.

What if I’m nervous about oral surgery?

Please tell us. We’ll walk through exactly what to expect, work at your pace, and discuss nitrous oxide sedation to help you feel more relaxed.

Is oral surgery covered by dental insurance?

Coverage varies by plan and procedure. Focus Dental Studio is in-network with most dental insurance plans, and our team can review your benefits and provide out-of-pocket estimates before any procedure. Financing through CareCredit® is also available.
