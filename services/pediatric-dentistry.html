Our Pediatric Services

Healthy Smiles from the Start

Gentle Exams & Cleanings

Regular checkups help children’s teeth develop well and catch problems early. We take a gentle, friendly approach and explain what we’re doing in kid-friendly terms, so children know what to expect.

Exams and cleanings are also a chance to teach brushing and flossing habits that last a lifetime.

Dental Fillings

Baby teeth matter. They hold space for adult teeth and help children chew and speak. When a child has a cavity, a filling removes the decay and restores the tooth so it stays healthy and comfortable.

We explain each step, numb the area, and take our time.

Tooth Extractions

Occasionally a tooth needs to be removed, for example when it is badly decayed, damaged by an injury, or not coming out on its own when it should. We make the experience as calm and comfortable as possible, explain what to expect, and give parents clear aftercare instructions.

Sealants & Fluoride

Sealants and fluoride are two effective ways to protect children’s teeth from cavities. Sealants shield the deep grooves of the back teeth, while fluoride strengthens tooth enamel. Both are quick and comfortable, and both are a smart part of a child’s preventive care.

Learn more about our preventative dentistry services.

Your Child’s First Visit

A first visit is usually short and low-pressure. We’ll look at your child’s teeth and gums, talk with you about brushing, diet, and habits like thumb sucking or pacifier use, and let your child get comfortable in our office.

A positive first experience sets the tone for years of healthy visits.

Common Questions

Pediatric Dentistry FAQs

When should my child first see the dentist?

Dental organizations generally recommend a first visit by a child’s first birthday. Early visits help children get comfortable and let us spot concerns sooner.

How can I help my child feel less nervous?

Talk about the visit in a positive, matter-of-fact way, and avoid words like “hurt” or “shot.” Bringing a favorite comfort item can help too. We’ll explain everything in kid-friendly terms and go at your child’s pace.

Do baby teeth really need fillings?

Yes. Baby teeth hold space for permanent teeth and help children chew and speak. An untreated cavity can cause pain and infection and can affect the teeth coming in behind it.

How often should my child have a checkup?

Most children do well with a checkup and cleaning about every six months. We’ll recommend a schedule based on your child’s needs.

Are sealants and fluoride safe for children?

Both are widely used and recommended by dental organizations to help prevent cavities. We’ll talk with you about what’s appropriate for your child’s age and cavity risk.

Does insurance cover children’s dental care?

Many dental plans include preventive care for children. Focus Dental Studio is in-network with most dental insurance plans, and our Focus Care membership offers reduced prices for families without insurance.
