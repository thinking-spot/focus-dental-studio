Our Cosmetic Services

Brighten, Repair, and Reshape

Dental Bonding

Dental bonding uses a tooth-colored resin that is shaped and hardened onto the tooth to repair chips and cracks, close small gaps, or improve the look of a discolored or uneven tooth. It is typically completed in a single visit and often requires little or no removal of natural tooth structure.

Dental Veneers

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells bonded to the front of your teeth to change their color, shape, size, or length. They can address stains that don’t respond to whitening, chips, gaps, and uneven teeth, and they’re a popular way to create a more uniform smile.

We’ll walk you through what the process involves and what to expect before you decide.

Teeth Whitening

Coffee, tea, red wine, tobacco, and the natural aging process can dull your smile over time. Professional teeth whitening can lighten stains and brighten your teeth, with the guidance of your dentist to help protect your teeth and gums.

Whitening works best after a checkup and cleaning, so we can confirm that your teeth and gums are healthy first.

Choosing the Right Treatment

Whitening brightens the color of natural teeth. Bonding can repair small chips and close minor gaps. Veneers can change the shape, size, and color of your front teeth. Many people combine more than one. Tell us what you’d like to change, and we’ll recommend the simplest option that gets you there.

Common Questions

Cosmetic Dentistry FAQs

What’s the difference between bonding and veneers?

Bonding uses tooth-colored resin built up and shaped directly on the tooth, usually in one visit, and is well suited to small repairs. Veneers are custom-made shells that cover the front of the tooth and can make larger changes to color, shape, and size.

How long do whitening results last?

It varies from person to person. Habits like drinking coffee, tea, or red wine, and using tobacco, can bring stains back sooner. Touch-ups can help maintain your results, and we’ll talk with you about what to expect.

Is professional teeth whitening safe?

When done with your dentist’s guidance, whitening is considered safe for most people. Some people notice temporary tooth sensitivity. We’ll check that your teeth and gums are healthy before you begin.

Will cosmetic treatments damage my natural teeth?

Whitening and bonding typically involve little or no removal of tooth structure. Veneers may involve removing a thin layer of enamel, and we’ll explain exactly what’s involved before you decide.

Does insurance cover cosmetic dentistry?

Coverage varies. Many plans treat purely cosmetic procedures as elective, so we’ll review your benefits and give you an estimate first. Focus Dental Studio is in-network with most dental insurance plans, and third-party financing through CareCredit® is also available.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

Start with a conversation. Call or text us to schedule a visit, tell us what you’d like to change, and we’ll examine your teeth and recommend the option that fits your goals and your budget.
