Our Restorative Services

Repair, Replace, and Restore

Same-Day Crowns (CEREC)

A crown covers and protects a tooth that is cracked, heavily filled, or weakened by decay. With CEREC technology, we design and create your crown right here in our office, so in most cases you can have your tooth restored in a single visit, with no temporary crown and no second appointment.

Same-day crowns also mean no weeks of waiting on a dental lab.

Dental Bridges

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring replacement teeth to the natural teeth or implants on either side of the gap. A bridge can restore your ability to chew and speak comfortably, keep neighboring teeth from drifting out of position, and fill in your smile.

Dental Fillings

When a tooth has a cavity, a filling removes the decayed area and restores the tooth’s shape and strength. Treating cavities early keeps them small and helps you avoid more extensive work later. We’ll talk with you about your filling options, including tooth-colored materials that blend in with your natural smile.

Dental Implant Restorations

A dental implant is a small post placed in the jawbone to act as a replacement tooth root. Once the implant has healed, a restoration such as a crown, bridge, or denture is attached to it, giving you a stable, natural-looking replacement for missing teeth.

We restore dental implants at Focus Dental Studio. The surgical placement of implants is part of our oral surgery services.

Dentures (Partial, Complete, Immediate)

Complete dentures replace all of the teeth on the upper or lower arch. Partial dentures fill the gaps when some natural teeth remain. Immediate dentures are placed on the day your teeth are removed, so you are never without teeth while your gums heal.

Dentures are made to fit comfortably and look natural. Immediate dentures may need adjustments as your gums heal, and we’ll make sure the fit and function feel right for you.

Root Canal Treatment

A root canal treats a tooth whose inner pulp has become infected or inflamed, which often causes a toothache, sensitivity to hot or cold, or swelling. During treatment, the infected tissue is removed, the inside of the tooth is cleaned and sealed, and the tooth is typically restored with a crown.

The goal is to relieve your pain and save your natural tooth rather than lose it. Most patients are comfortable during treatment, which is performed with local anesthesia.

Common Questions

Restorative Dentistry FAQs

What is a same-day crown?

A same-day crown is a permanent crown designed and made in our office with CEREC technology. In most cases, that means you can have your tooth restored in a single visit, without a temporary crown or a second appointment.

How long do fillings and crowns last?

It depends on the material, the location of the tooth, your bite, and how well you care for your teeth. With good home care and regular checkups, fillings and crowns can last many years, and we’ll monitor them at your exams.

Do I need to replace a missing tooth?

In most cases, yes. A gap can allow neighboring teeth to shift, make chewing more difficult, and affect your bite over time. Bridges, implants, and dentures are all options, and we’ll help you weigh them.

Does a root canal hurt?

Most patients are comfortable during a root canal, which is performed with local anesthesia. The pain people associate with root canals usually comes from the infection itself, and treatment is meant to relieve it.

What’s the difference between a bridge, an implant, and a denture?

A bridge uses the teeth on either side of a gap to hold a replacement tooth in place. An implant replaces the tooth root with a post in the jawbone and supports a crown, bridge, or denture. A denture is a removable set of replacement teeth. The best choice depends on your teeth, gums, and goals.

How can I pay for restorative treatment?

Focus Dental Studio is in-network with most dental insurance plans, and our team can review your benefits and provide out-of-pocket estimates before any procedure. We also offer third-party financing with CareCredit®, and our Focus Care membership provides reduced prices for patients without insurance.
