About Sedation

Nitrous Oxide, Explained

Nitrous Oxide (“Laughing Gas”)

Nitrous oxide is a mild sedative gas that you breathe through a small mask over your nose. It helps you feel relaxed and less anxious while you stay awake and able to respond. It is a well-established, commonly used form of dental sedation.

How It Works

You breathe a mixture of nitrous oxide and oxygen, and its calming effects begin within a few minutes. The amount is adjusted throughout your visit to keep you comfortable.

When your procedure is finished, you breathe pure oxygen for a few minutes to clear the gas from your system.

Who It’s a Good Fit For

Nitrous oxide can help people who feel dental anxiety or fear, who have a sensitive gag reflex, or who find it hard to sit still for longer visits. It’s often a good option when you’d like to feel more relaxed but don’t need to be deeply sedated.

We’ll review your health history to confirm that it’s appropriate for you.

What to Expect

Most people feel calm and relaxed, and some feel lightheaded or a little tingly. The effects wear off quickly once the gas is stopped, and in most cases patients can go about their day afterward, including driving themselves home.

Please tell us about any health conditions or medications beforehand, so we can be sure it’s right for you.

Common Questions

Sedation Dentistry FAQs

Is nitrous oxide safe?

Nitrous oxide has a long track record of safe use in dentistry when it’s given properly. We review your health history first to make sure it’s a good choice for you.

Will I be asleep?

No. With nitrous oxide you stay awake and able to respond, but you feel relaxed and calm.

How quickly does it wear off, and can I drive afterward?

The effects wear off within minutes once you’re breathing oxygen again. In most cases patients can drive themselves home and carry on with their day, and we’ll confirm what’s right for you at your visit.

Which dental visits can I use it for?

Nitrous oxide can be used with many types of dental care. Mention it when you schedule so we can plan your visit around it.

What if I’m very anxious about the dentist?

Tell us when you call. We can take extra time, explain each step before we do it, and use nitrous oxide to help you feel more at ease. You’re never the only nervous patient we’ve seen.

Is sedation covered by insurance?

Coverage for sedation varies by plan. Call or text us and we’ll review your benefits and give you an estimate before your visit.
