Your Dentist

Misook An Rissler, DDS

Putting Your Family First

Dr. Rissler is a friendly and approachable dentist who genuinely enjoys getting to know her patients. With every appointment, she helps her patients feel comfortable and safe by building trust, rapport, and an honest, professional relationship. Her rich experience with patients of all ages makes her an ideal family dentist — and she looks forward to providing lifelong dental care to your entire family.

Dr. Rissler strives to provide the most up-to-date technology and high-quality treatment through ongoing continuing education. Outside the office, she loves singing in her church choir and spending quality time with her two daughters.

Born and raised in South Korea, Dr. Rissler came to the United States to further her education. She is a graduate of the Red Cross College of Nursing in Seoul, South Korea; Pittsburg State University in Pittsburg, KS; and the University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Dentistry.
